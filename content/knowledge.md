## Process

1. The visitor sends photos or a floor plan on WhatsApp or the contact form.
2. Free home visit: the team measures every wall and opening.
3. 3D design in the visitor's own room, free. Anything can be changed. Nothing is built until approved.
4. Fixed quotation before production. Made in the Dubai workshop. Installed by the company's own fitters, who align
   doors and lights and clear packaging.
